Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

What we process

ClipForge processes the media URL you submit and temporary media data required to produce your requested file. Processing is performed by the local application server. The application does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment information.

Temporary files

Downloaded files are stored in a temporary server directory only while your response is being prepared. The temporary directory is scheduled for deletion after the response completes. Browser quality preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage and are not sent to the server.

Third-party services

The application uses yt-dlp extractors to request public media metadata and streams from supported platforms. The interface loads Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, and fonts from third-party CDNs, which may receive standard browser request data. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your responsibility

Only submit links and download content you are authorized to access and save. Do not use the application to evade access controls, download private material, or infringe copyright.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
